fix(sheets): increase tail block length limit from 4 to 12 chars

_KIT_CODE_PATTERN now allows up to 12 characters in tail blocks
(?:-[A-ZА-ЯЁ0-9.]{1,12}) instead of {1,4}. This allows 3 previously
rejected kits to pass: ГИС-РД-СБ-ССООИ-0-00, АТ-РД-АПТ-ГК.00, and
the base part of дробный шифр ГИС-РД-СБ-ССООИ-0-00/10511.25.01-ССООИ.
